Liyora Keyboard Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Liyora Keyboard works on your phone. What you type is never sent to us or to anyone else, and there are no accounts to create.

What stays on your device

To work properly, the keyboard saves a few things on your phone only:

  • Your settings, such as theme, key size and the languages you turned on
  • Clipboard history and the items you pin
  • Recently used emoji
  • Words the keyboard learned from you and words you added to your own dictionary
  • Live wallpapers you downloaded

None of this leaves your device. We cannot see it, and we do not collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. Liyora Keyboard does not collect, sell or share personal information, and it does not need you to sign up.

Permissions

The only permission the keyboard asks for is the microphone, and only for voice typing. It is optional. If you never use the mic key, you can leave it turned off.

Liyora does not ask for your contacts, photos, camera, files or location.

Voice typing

The microphone is used only after you tap the mic key. Liyora does not record or store your voice. Speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service built into your Android phone, and that service follows its own provider's privacy policy.

Internet use

The keyboard connects to the internet for one reason: to download the live wallpapers you choose. These come from a public GitHub repository, so GitHub can see the request, including your IP address, as it does for any download. The request never includes anything you typed.

Password fields and private typing

  • In password fields, suggestions, learning and clipboard history are turned off, and nothing is saved.
  • When an app asks keyboards not to learn from a field, Liyora respects that.
  • Incognito mode stops the keyboard from learning words and saving recent emoji until you turn it off.

Clipboard

Liyora shows text you copied so you can paste it again later. You can clear the history at any time, or turn on auto clear to remove it after 1 minute, 5 minutes or 1 hour.

Deleting your data

Everything Liyora stores lives on your phone. You can remove it by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the keyboard.

Children

Liyora Keyboard does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and describe what changed in the app's update notes.
